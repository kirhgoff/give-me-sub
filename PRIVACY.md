# GiveMeSub Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026.

GiveMeSub shows live captions for audio playing on your Mac. It is built to work entirely on your device.

## What the app accesses

- **System audio.** With your permission (macOS "System Audio Recording"), GiveMeSub listens to the audio your Mac plays, only while captions are on. The menu bar icon is solid while it is listening.
- **Settings.** Your choices (language, caption appearance, caption mode, launch at login) are saved in the app's preferences on your Mac.

## What happens to it

- Audio is transcribed on your Mac by a speech model bundled with the app.
- Audio and captions are kept in memory only while shown, and are never saved to disk.
- Nothing is sent over the network. GiveMeSub has no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and shares no data with third parties.

## Your control

Turn captions off at any time from the menu bar, with ⌃⌥⌘C, or in Shortcuts. You can revoke audio access in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen & System Audio Recording. Deleting the app removes its preferences.

## Children

GiveMeSub collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

Updates to this policy are published in this file; the effective date above changes with them.
